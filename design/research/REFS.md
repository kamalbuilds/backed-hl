# BACKED research: references (captured with refshot-pw, a Playwright port of ui-craft refshot.py; bhn deepsurge hangs on this Mac)

Every entry was captured at 1440 and 390, the PNG opened and looked at, and tokens read from `refs/<name>.tokens.json` before writing the entry. Pilot was hl-trade, hl-vaults, flighty, ramp (ramp 404, replaced). Domain: 7. Outside the domain, picked by problem shape ("someone else's money with a hard limit that acts by itself", "an incident record with a source"): 4.

## hl-trade: Hyperliquid testnet ETH-USDC trade screen (north star, product screen 1)
What it is: the venue BACKED's vault trades on. Four panels touch with 1px gaps: chart left, book centre, ticket right, positions/blotter under the chart. Move worth stealing: the market strip above the chart is label-over-value pairs (Mark 2,484.1 / Oracle 2,497.2 / 24h Change), all 12px OurFont with 31px control height; the Connect button is the only filled teal rgb(80,210,193) at radius 8px, padding 0 16px. Numbers are plain, colour carries sign only (-6.3 / -0.25% in red). Empty state "No open positions yet" sits in the blotter, left aligned, no illustration. What NOT to take: the whole terminal grid (every perp DEX ships it, and our previous BACKED was exactly this, see negative/), TradingView chrome, the announcements toast. PNG: refs/hl-trade-1440.png, refs/hl-trade-390.png

## hl-vaults: Hyperliquid vault index (north star, product screen 2)
What it is: list of vaults with Leader, APR, TVL, Your Deposit, Age, Snapshot sparkline. Move worth stealing: one big figure only where it is the page's subject (Total Value Locked $275,911,237 in a panel at roughly 28px), then a dense table with 12px rows on rgb(4,37,31) deep green body over html rgb(15,26,30). Addresses are truncated 0x7789...f60d and never wrap. APR coloured green/red, nothing else coloured. What NOT to take: sparklines as decoration in every row (ui-craft research protocol lists sparklines as fake impact), the faint concentric background arcs. PNG: refs/hl-vaults-1440.png, refs/hl-vaults-390.png

## hl-hlp: Hyperliquid HLP vault detail (north star, product screen 3)
What it is: the closest existing thing to a BACKED vault page: breadcrumb "Vaults > Hyperliquidity Provider (HLP)", address with copy, four stat panels, About/Performance tabs, a stepped PNL chart, then Positions (177) blotter. Move worth stealing: the PNL chart is a stepped white line on dark with a plain 0 / 250,000 / 500,000 axis, no gradient fill; tabs are text with a 1px underline; the blotter shows leverage as "20x" next to the coin with a 3px red/green left bar per row. Buttons Withdraw/Deposit are outline at radius 8px, 12px text. What NOT to take: the four equal stat panels (the big-metric template), the dark green theme itself (it is Hyperliquid's brand, BACKED must not look like a reskin of HL). PNG: refs/hl-hlp-1440.png, refs/hl-hlp-390.png

## lighter-trade: Lighter ETH perp trade screen
What it is: perp DEX terminal. Move worth stealing: the bottom status bar "ONLINE / MAINNET / WALLET NOT CONNECTED / BLOCK 352,752,344" set in Geist Mono Variable small caps, which turns system state into one line of text instead of badges; body text Inter Variable 14px weight 485 with -0.28px tracking; buttons radius 4px, padding 0 8.8px. Empty state "NOT CONNECTED / Connect a wallet to see your open positions / Connect Wallet" is a centred three-line stack inside the blotter. What NOT to take: Inter, the mono-caps everywhere (letter-spaced mono caps on every label is a CWF ban), the order book. PNG: refs/lighter-trade-1440.png, refs/lighter-trade-390.png

## aevo-trade: Aevo ETH perpetual
What it is: perp terminal with a ticket on the right. Move worth stealing: the ticket groups risk numbers (Required Margin, Order Value, Liquidation Price, Slippage, Fees) as a two-column label/value list under the action, values right aligned, 14px BaseFont, body rgb(6,6,12); the footer ticker says "Systems Operational" as plain text. Buttons BananaGrotesk 14px on rgba(14,14,20,0.92) radius 6px padding 8px. What NOT to take: neon green Buy outline, the scrolling ticker tape, rewards badge. PNG: refs/aevo-trade-1440.png, refs/aevo-trade-390.png

## hyperdash: Hyperdash terminal on Hyperliquid data
What it is: a third-party HL terminal. Move worth stealing: a warm near-black rgb(20,18,16) instead of the usual blue-black, BDO Grotesk 12px weight 500 with 0.22px tracking on figures, one loud accent (orange) used only on the primary action "Trade S&P500" and the logo mark; inputs rgba(255,255,255,0.04) radius 4px. Shows a single orange-red primary per screen. What NOT to take: the welcome video modal, the pill "RFQ NEW" badge, uppercase nav. PNG: refs/hyperdash-1440.png, refs/hyperdash-390.png

## drift-trade: Drift ETH-PERP (captured while Drift is paused)
What it is: perp terminal, captured during a protocol pause, with a banner "Drift is being paused until further notice" and an RPC Connection Error toast. Move worth stealing: how a paused/halted venue states its state: a full-width 14px banner at the very top, plain sentence, with the reason and where to look; the ticket greys out but stays visible. That is BACKED's FLATTENED/frozen state. Body rgb(3,3,5), Inter 14px 600, buttons padding 4px 12px radius 0px. What NOT to take: purple Connect Wallet button, skeleton grey blocks while loading (loading-first), the empty chart. PNG: refs/drift-trade-1440.png, refs/drift-trade-390.png

## stripe-controls: Stripe Issuing, spending controls (outside: limits on someone else's spending)
What it is: the docs page for card spending controls: allowed_categories, blocked_categories, spending_limits as a PARAMETER / TYPE / DESCRIPTION table. Move worth stealing: the rules are the product and they are shown as a parameter table with the literal parameter names in a 13px code chip, h1 32px 700 rgb(26,31,54), body 16px/26px rgb(60,66,87), right rail "On this page". A backer's rules (allowed markets, max leverage, loss line, daily loss) read exactly like this. What NOT to take: the docs left nav, blue links, "Ask AI" chrome. PNG: refs/stripe-controls-1440.png, refs/stripe-controls-390.png

## vercel-spend: Vercel Spend Management docs (outside: a hard cap that acts automatically)
What it is: explains a spend amount that triggers actions: notify, webhook, pause production. Move worth stealing: the consequence is stated as a short list of actions, then a single amber callout "Setting a spend amount does not stop usage on its own. To stop incurring costs, turn on Pause Production Deployments". h1 GeistSans 40px 600 with -2.4px tracking, h3 20px 600 -0.4px, body 16px/27.2px rgb(23,23,23) on rgb(250,250,250). Negative tracking on display, airy body. What NOT to take: Geist (Vercel's face, and already in the design log), the docs chrome. PNG: refs/vercel-spend-1440.png, refs/vercel-spend-390.png

## gh-status: GitHub Status incident history (outside: an incident record with a source)
What it is: month-grouped incidents, each a red title, a one-sentence resolution and a time window "Oct 7, 17:17 - 18:04 UTC", hung on a 1px vertical rail with a circle node. Move worth stealing: the incident log as the proof surface: h2 month 28px 500, entries 14px/21px, the duration as plain text, colour only on the title. BACKED's breaches are incidents: "Loss line crossed, flattened in 2.05s, tx". Body rgb(246,248,250), text rgb(36,41,46). What NOT to take: the illustrated hard-hat banner, "Subscribe to Updates", FontAwesome icons. PNG: refs/gh-status-1440.png, refs/gh-status-390.png

## flighty: Flighty (outside: "get the truth" about a live event against a schedule)
What it is: flight tracker marketing page; the phone shows My Flights with "1h / San Francisco to New York / Departs On Time" rows. Move worth stealing: state words carry the meaning ("On Time", "Gate changed", "Landed 22m early") and the time delta is the headline of each row; h1 65px 700 with -1px tracking, 78px leading. The idea for BACKED: lead each event with the delta ("2.05s") the way Flighty leads with "22m early". What NOT to take: the hand-holding-phone hero, floating notification cards, EB Garamond/Indie Flower, the cookie banner. PNG: refs/flighty-1440.png, refs/flighty-390.png
