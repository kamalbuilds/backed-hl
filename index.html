      What happens to your order

      1. You sign a tx to trade(), or an EIP-712 order any relayer submits with tradeBySig().
      2. The contract checks market list, aggregate notional of all positions at the oracle price ≤ max leverage × capital, equity above the loss line.
      3. Only then it sends the IOC to HyperCore through CoreWriter (≈0.4 s queue, p50 412 ms measured).
      4. No one, including us, holds a key that can place an order for this account.
